# Пані Анна

Live site: https://anna.hotelup.work

## About
Пані Анна is a hotel and restaurant complex located in the village of Смотрич (Smotrych), on the road toward Chernivtsi, right past the "Лань, що біжить" bridge. The address is вул. Героїв Майдану, с. Смотрич. The property combines a large banquet hall (up to 300 guests), a Ukrainian-cuisine restaurant that operates by pre-order, and on-site accommodation for event guests, positioning itself as a single-location venue for weddings, anniversaries, corporate events, family celebrations, and graduation parties.

## Amenities
- Banquet hall with a stated capacity of up to 300 guests
- Ukrainian cuisine restaurant (by pre-order/reservation)
- On-site guest accommodation (hotel rooms) for event attendees
- Event planning support for weddings, anniversaries, corporate events, family celebrations, and graduations
- Online event request form with a summary generator for calling in details
